A separate commercial license is available if you want to ship a proprietary
product without the AGPL's source-sharing obligations.

## Who needs a commercial license?

You need a commercial license if **all** of the following are true:

- You are building a product that you ship to customers (SaaS, desktop app,
  kiosk, embedded UI, internal tooling sold or licensed to third parties)
- You do not want to open-source your product under AGPL-3.0
- Your use is not covered by the AGPL's personal or research exemptions

If you are building an open-source project, a personal project, or a research
prototype, AGPL-3.0 covers you at no cost.

## What the commercial license covers

- Use of `@effindomv2/fui-as` in proprietary, closed-source products
- No obligation to publish your product's source code
- Pricing is scoped to your project — there is no one-size-fits-all rate
